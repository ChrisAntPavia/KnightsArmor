# Knights Armor

**A student-run, map-based campus awareness app for UCF, built by students for students.**

UCF is one of the largest universities in the country, and that scale brings disruptions and safety concerns: police activity, protests, construction, gas leaks and severe weather, often with little or no warning. Right now that information is scattered across UCF's official sites, social media and Reddit, so it's hard to find when it matters most.

Knights Armor puts it all in one place. Students can report and view incidents in real time with photos and GPS pins, track official alerts and weather threats, and find planned campus events, all on a single map-based feed.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Knights Armor is a student convenience tool. It does **not** replace UCF's official services or emergency alert system. In an emergency, use official channels such as **UCF Alert** and **UCFPD**.# Knights Armor

## Features

### Base features
- **Incident Reporting:** Report what's happening on campus (an ambulance, a gas leak, a protest) with a category, description, optional photo and GPS location, so others know what to avoid.
- **Interactive Campus Map:** See real-time, color-coded pins from user reports and filter them by category to show only what you care about.
- **Weather Tracker:** Get weather information alongside everything else, with flags for likely disruptions such as extreme heat or expected downpours.

### Possible extras
- **Campus Event Tracker:** Keep up with what UCF offers students, such as Wednesday Market, concerts and sports.
- **Garage Tracker:** See UCF parking garage availability.
- **Classroom Tracker:** Find open classrooms for studying or student organization meetings.
- **Amenity Tracker:** Find bathrooms, water fountains, microwaves and other essentials around campus.
